1 Appointment and Territory

PARROT CANADA VISA CONSULTANT CO.LTD appoints CCN COMPANY as its designated local representative for Burundi, subject to this Agreement. CCN accepts the appointment and shall represent the Parrot brand professionally, lawfully and only within the authority expressly granted here.

Clients originating in, residing in or requiring local support in Burundi shall ordinarily be referred to CCN for local orientation, registration support, document collection, follow-up and communication. Parrot retains control of its professional service standards, institutional relationships, application strategy and final acceptance of every file.

This appointment does not create a branch, franchise, employment relationship, joint venture, power of attorney or authority for CCN to bind Parrot to a debt, guarantee, refund, immigration result or third-party contract unless Parrot gives prior written authorization.

2 Scope of the Partnership

Promote Parrot's approved education and mobility services in Burundi using current, authorized information and branding.

Receive and orient prospective clients referred by Parrot or generated by CCN in Burundi.

Complete preliminary screening and collect clear copies of passports, academic records, language results, financial evidence and other approved documents.

Register every client and material case update in the approved Parrot MIS or referral system and maintain accurate communication records.

Explain only approved service descriptions, fee schedules, refund terms and document requirements.

Coordinate admission files and study-permit administrative support with Parrot and, when regulated Canadian immigration advice is required, with an appropriately authorized Canadian representative.

Refer potential work-permit candidates to Parrot for case-specific assessment before discussing price or accepting funds.

CCN shall not change Parrot's normal fee, invent a discount, collect an unapproved amount or promise that a fee is refundable unless the applicable client contract expressly says so.

4 Work Permit Cases and Third Parties

Work-permit opportunities are case-specific and may depend on an available genuine job offer, employer requirements, occupation, location, language level, candidate eligibility and terms imposed by a third-party recruitment or immigration-service provider. No general work-permit price is created by this Agreement.

Parrot and CCN shall discuss and approve the opportunity, third party, total fee, payment milestones, refund terms and responsibilities separately for each available job offer.

No work-permit fee may be advertised, promised or collected until Parrot issues or approves a written case quotation or client agreement.

The identity and role of every third party must be disclosed to the client before payment, together with any important conditions or non-refundable amounts.

Neither company may describe a job offer, work permit or visa as guaranteed. Employers, authorized representatives and government authorities make their own decisions.

5 Client Referral and File Ownership

CCN shall create a referral record for each client before or at first formal onboarding. The record must show the client's full name, contact details, selected service, referral date and responsible CCN staff member. A client is treated as a CCN-supported Burundi referral only when the record is entered in the approved system or acknowledged in writing by Parrot.

Client personal information and case records may be used only for the agreed service, lawful compliance and necessary internal administration. Neither party owns the client as property. Each party shall respect the client's right to choose, withdraw, request records and communicate directly with institutions or authorized professionals where permitted.

6 CCN Responsibilities

Maintain a lawful and professional office at the stated Burundi address and promptly notify Parrot of any change.

Assign trained staff and ensure that every person communicating with clients identifies CCN and its relationship with Parrot accurately.

Use Parrot's name, logo and materials only as approved and stop using them immediately when this Agreement ends.

Provide accurate information, avoid misleading advertising and make no guarantee of admission, scholarship, job offer, visa, work permit, processing time or entry to another country.

Verify that client information is complete and promptly escalate suspected fraud, altered documents, undisclosed refusals, criminal concerns or material inconsistencies.

Issue or obtain an official receipt for every payment and ensure all payments and receipts are recorded in the Parrot MIS or approved accounting record.

Protect client documents, credentials and personal information and report any suspected data or payment-security incident immediately.

Comply with Burundi law, consumer-protection rules, advertising requirements and all lawful instructions issued by Parrot.

7 Parrot Responsibilities

Provide current service descriptions, approved fee schedules, application requirements, partner instructions and reasonable training for CCN staff.

Review referred files, confirm whether Parrot accepts the case and communicate material requirements, deadlines and decisions.

Coordinate with schools, approved partners and authorized professionals within the scope of the client's written service agreement.

Provide CCN with reasonable status updates that may lawfully be shared with the client.

Maintain records of amounts received by Parrot and issue or arrange official receipts.

Notify CCN when a fee, policy, institution requirement, third-party condition or service is changed or withdrawn.

8 Payments Receipts and Reconciliation

A client shall pay only through an account or method officially approved in writing. CCN shall not direct a client to a staff member's personal account, an undisclosed third party, cryptocurrency or another unapproved recipient.

If Parrot authorizes CCN to collect a client payment, CCN shall issue an approved receipt, enter the payment in the MIS on the same business day, safeguard the amount, and remit it according to written payment instructions. The parties shall reconcile referred clients, invoices, receipts, refunds and outstanding balances at least monthly.

A party disputing a reconciliation entry must notify the other party in writing within fifteen business days after receiving the statement. This time limit does not validate fraud, misuse of funds or an unlawful charge.

9 Immigration Advice and Professional Boundaries

CCN and Parrot act as education, referral and administrative service providers within their lawful authority. Neither party may provide regulated Canadian immigration advice or representation for payment unless the service is delivered by an authorized Canadian representative under the required retainer, consent and regulatory rules.

CCN shall refer legal, admissibility, refusal, misrepresentation and complex immigration questions to Parrot for routing to the appropriate authorized professional. CCN shall not sign an immigration form as representative, submit itself as an authorized representative, or use another professional's licence without written authority.

10 Brand Marketing and Public Statements

CCN may identify itself as the authorized Burundi representative of Parrot only during the term of this Agreement and only in the approved form. Marketing materials using either company's logo, fee, success claim, job offer or immigration statement require prior written approval from the other affected party.

Neither party may publish false approval rates, fabricated testimonials, unavailable jobs, misleading scholarships or statements implying government endorsement. Client images or testimonials require the client's informed written consent.

11 Confidentiality and Data Protection

Each party shall keep confidential all non-public client, financial, operational, institutional, partner and pricing information received under this Agreement. Disclosure is limited to persons who need the information for the agreed service and are bound by appropriate confidentiality duties.

Each party shall apply reasonable security measures, limit access, protect passwords, retain records only as required, and promptly notify the other party of loss, unauthorized access, altered payment instructions or suspected fraud. These duties survive termination.

12 Compliance Audit and Suspension

Parrot may reasonably review referral records, receipts, approved marketing and MIS entries relating to this partnership. CCN shall correct a documented deficiency promptly. Parrot may immediately suspend new referrals, brand use or payment collection when there is suspected fraud, misuse of funds, serious client harm, unlawful representation, data breach or material misrepresentation.

13 Term Renewal and Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause by giving thirty days' written notice. Either party may terminate immediately for fraud, misuse of client funds, unlawful conduct, serious confidentiality breach, unauthorized immigration representation, material brand misuse, insolvency or a material breach not corrected within ten business days after written notice when correction is possible.

On termination, CCN shall stop representing itself as Parrot's Burundi representative, stop using Parrot branding, return or securely transfer active client files, reconcile all payments and cooperate in an orderly handover. Existing client obligations, earned fees, refunds, confidentiality, records and dispute provisions survive as necessary.

14 Liability and No Guarantee

Each party is responsible for its own acts, omissions, staff, representations and handling of funds. Neither party is liable for an independent decision of a school, employer, bank, government authority, authorized professional or disclosed third party, except to the extent loss is caused by that party's proven breach, negligence, fraud or unauthorized act.

No provision guarantees admission, a loan, scholarship, job offer, study permit, work permit, visa, processing time, entry, employment or refund from an independent third party.

15 Notices Disputes and Governing Principles

Notices must be delivered by acknowledged email, registered mail, courier or hand delivery to the contact details in this Agreement or a later written notice. The parties shall first attempt good-faith negotiation between their managing representatives and may agree to mediation before court proceedings.

This cross-border Agreement shall be interpreted consistently with mandatory laws applicable in Rwanda and Burundi and with mandatory rules governing any service performed in another country. The parties shall record in the signature section the agreed court or arbitration forum before signing. Nothing removes a client's or party's right to approach a competent regulator or authority.

16 Entire Agreement and Changes

This Agreement and any signed schedules contain the entire partnership agreement on the stated subject and replace earlier oral or written understandings. A fee change, commission schedule, exclusivity term, work-permit arrangement, expanded authority or other amendment is valid only when written and signed or electronically accepted by authorized representatives of both companies. If one provision is unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue to the extent permitted by law.

Signatures

By signing, each party confirms that its representative is authorized, that the Agreement has been reviewed and understood, and that each party will receive an identical signed copy.
