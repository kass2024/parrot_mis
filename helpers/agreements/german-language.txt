ARTICLE 2: PURPOSE OF THE AGREEMENT
The purpose of this agreement is to establish a B2B partnership for recruiting and assisting eligible international students who wish to travel to Germany to study the German language through face-to-face classes at recognized or lawfully operating German language schools.
The partnership is specifically intended for students seeking admission to German language courses conducted physically in Germany.
The Parties agree that this contract is exclusively for in-person German language study programs in Germany.
It does not cover employment placement, vocational training, Canadian immigration, or other international study and immigration services.
ARTICLE 3: SCOPE OF SERVICES
PARROT shall provide the following services to eligible students referred by THE PARTNER:
Preliminary assessment of the student's eligibility for German language studies.
Identification of suitable German language schools in Germany.
Guidance on available German language course levels, including A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, and C2, where offered.
Assistance with choosing appropriate in-person language courses.
Review of the student's admission documents.
Preparation and submission of applications to German language schools.
Communication and follow-up with language schools.
Assistance in obtaining an official admission or enrollment confirmation letter.
Provision of information concerning language course tuition fees, duration, and commencement dates.
Administrative assistance with visa application documentation and appointment preparation.
Coordination of supporting documents required by the relevant German embassy or consulate.
Follow-up on application progress and communication of relevant updates.
The Parties acknowledge that visa eligibility depends on the specific language course, its intensity and duration, the applicant's circumstances, and the applicable German immigration rules.
Any regulated immigration advice or representation shall be provided only by appropriately authorized professionals.
ARTICLE 4: TOTAL SERVICE FEE AND PAYMENT SCHEDULE
The Parties agree that the total service fee payable to PARROT for each accepted candidate shall be:
TOTAL SERVICE FEE: €2,000 (TWO THOUSAND EUROS) PER CANDIDATE.
4.1 Payment Schedule
First installment: €250
Payable before starting the German language school admission process.
Second installment: €250

Third installment: €1,500
Payable only after official German visa approval.
TOTAL SERVICE FEE: €2,000 PER CANDIDATE
4.2 Excluded Expenses
Unless otherwise expressly agreed in writing, the €2,000 service fee does not include:
German language school tuition fees.
School registration or enrollment fees.
German embassy or consulate visa fees.
Health or travel insurance.
Accommodation and living expenses.
Blocked account deposits or other proof-of-funds requirements.
Flight tickets.
Translation and legalization expenses.
Other third-party charges.
All applicable external expenses shall be communicated to THE PARTNER before payment is required.
ARTICLE 5: FIRST INSTALLMENT — €250 AND REFUND CONDITIONS
THE PARTNER shall pay the first installment of €250 before PARROT begins the German language school admission process.
This installment covers the initial assessment, document review, school selection, application preparation, and admission coordination.
5.1 Refund Eligibility
The first installment of €250 shall be refundable if PARROT fails to obtain an official admission or enrollment confirmation letter from a German language school, provided that:
The candidate satisfies the admission requirements disclosed before processing.
The candidate submits all necessary documents.
All submitted documents are authentic and complete.
The candidate cooperates with the admission process.
The candidate does not voluntarily withdraw before completion of the agreed admission process.
If PARROT cannot obtain admission under these conditions, the €250 shall be refunded within 15 business days after the unsuccessful admission outcome is confirmed.
Once an official admission or enrollment confirmation letter is obtained, the first installment becomes non-refundable, except where applicable law requires otherwise.
5.2 Admission Letter Requirements
The admission or enrollment confirmation must be genuine, issued by the relevant language school, and correspond to the candidate's agreed course.
An admission letter alone does not guarantee that the candidate qualifies for a German visa.
ARTICLE 6: SECOND INSTALLMENT — €250 AND NON-REFUNDABLE CONDITIONS
THE PARTNER shall pay the second installment of €250 before submission of the German language study visa application.
This installment covers agreed visa documentation preparation, administrative review, and application coordination.
6.1 Non-Refundable Condition
The second installment of €250 is non-refundable once the agreed visa preparation services have commenced, subject to applicable law.
This includes situations where:
The German visa application is refused.
The candidate withdraws after visa preparation has commenced.
The candidate changes their decision to travel or study.
The embassy requests additional documentation.
The visa application takes longer than expected.
This provision does not exclude mandatory refund rights arising from non-performance, material breach, or applicable consumer protection laws.
ARTICLE 7: THIRD INSTALLMENT — €1,500 AFTER VISA APPROVAL
The final installment of €1,500 shall become payable only after the candidate receives official approval of the relevant German visa.
THE PARTNER agrees to pay the outstanding balance within five business days after receiving verifiable confirmation of visa approval.
If the German visa application is refused, the final €1,500 shall not become payable.
Visa approval must be supported by official or otherwise verifiable confirmation from the relevant German authority.
ARTICLE 8: GERMAN LANGUAGE STUDY REQUIREMENTS
The Parties acknowledge that candidates may be required to provide:
A valid passport.
An official language school admission or enrollment confirmation.
Evidence of the language course schedule and duration.
Proof of sufficient financial resources.
Proof of appropriate health insurance.
Accommodation information, where required.
A motivation letter explaining the purpose of studying German.
Academic or employment history documents, where relevant.
Evidence of previous German language studies, if required for the selected course or visa category.
Any additional documents requested by the relevant German diplomatic mission.
The exact requirements shall depend on the candidate's circumstances, country of application, and current German immigration rules.

Explain the nature of the face-to-face German language programs.
Submit complete and authentic candidate documents.
Provide accurate candidate information.
Obtain necessary consent before sharing personal information.
Pay the agreed service fees according to Article 4.
Inform candidates about applicable school tuition and visa-related expenses.
Cooperate with PARROT throughout the admission and visa application process.
Avoid providing false or misleading information.
Avoid guaranteeing visa approval.

ARTICLE 12: REFUND AND CANCELLATION POLICY
The Parties expressly agree to the following conditions.
12.1 First Installment — €250

12.2 Second Installment — €250
Non-refundable once the agreed visa preparation services commence, subject to applicable law.
12.3 Third Installment — €1,500
Payable only after official German visa approval.
Not payable if the visa application is refused.
12.4 Candidate Withdrawal
Where a candidate voluntarily withdraws, any refund shall be determined according to the services already performed, the applicable written terms, and mandatory legal requirements.
12.5 Refund Request Procedure
Refund requests shall be submitted in writing with the candidate's application reference and proof of payment.
PARROT shall review and respond to refund requests in accordance with this agreement and applicable law.
ARTICLE 13: CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA PROTECTION
Both Parties agree to maintain the confidentiality of candidate information, including passports, academic records, financial documents, application records, and other personal information.
Candidate information shall be used only for legitimate admission and visa support purposes.
Neither Party shall disclose confidential information to unauthorized third parties.
Any transfer of personal information to German language schools or relevant service providers shall comply with applicable privacy laws and consent requirements.
ARTICLE 14: NO GUARANTEE OF ADMISSION OR VISA APPROVAL
The Parties acknowledge that:
Admission decisions are made by the respective German language schools.
Visa decisions are made by the relevant German authorities.
Processing times may vary.
Admission does not guarantee visa approval.
Visa approval does not automatically guarantee permission to work or remain permanently in Germany.
Neither Party shall advertise or promise guaranteed visas, employment, or immigration status.
ARTICLE 15: SCOPE LIMITATION
This agreement applies only to the recruitment, admission processing, and visa application support of candidates seeking to attend in-person German language courses in Germany.
The following services are expressly excluded:
Canadian immigration applications.
Express Entry and permanent residence services.
Spousal sponsorship and work permits.
Provincial Nominee Program applications.
Francophone Mobility work permits.
European employment recruitment or job placement.
University degree admissions outside the agreed German language study program.
Other international visa and immigration services.
Any additional service shall require a separate written agreement signed by both Parties.
ARTICLE 16: NON-EXCLUSIVITY
Unless otherwise agreed in writing, this agreement is non-exclusive.
Both Parties may cooperate with other educational institutions or recruitment partners, provided they respect their contractual obligations and confidentiality requirements.
ARTICLE 17: DURATION AND TERMINATION
This agreement shall become effective upon signature by both Parties.
The initial duration shall be one year.
The agreement may be renewed by mutual written consent.
Either Party may terminate the agreement by providing 30 days' written notice.
Either Party may terminate the agreement earlier in the event of serious breach, fraud, or unlawful conduct, subject to applicable law.
Termination shall not eliminate lawful outstanding payment obligations, accrued refund rights, or responsibilities relating to active applications.
ARTICLE 18: INDEPENDENT BUSINESS RELATIONSHIP
Both Parties remain independent legal entities.
Nothing in this agreement creates an employment relationship, joint venture, or authority for either Party to make commitments on behalf of the other without written authorization.
Each Party remains responsible for its own business operations and legal compliance.
