2. PURPOSE AND PROGRAM SCOPE

This Agreement governs PARROT's direct provision of admission and administrative support to the Student for an in-person German language course at a recognized or lawfully operating language school in Germany.

This Agreement applies only to German language studies in Germany. It does not include employment placement, vocational training, university degree admission, Canadian immigration, permanent residence, or any service not expressly stated in writing.

3. SERVICES PROVIDED BY PARROT

Preliminary assessment of the Student's profile for German language study.

Identification of suitable German language schools and available levels, including A1 to C2 where offered.

Assistance in choosing an appropriate in-person course.

Review of admission documents and preparation and submission of school applications.

Communication and follow-up with the selected language school.

Assistance in obtaining a genuine admission or enrollment confirmation.

Information concerning tuition fees, course duration and commencement dates.

Administrative assistance with visa documents and appointment preparation.

Coordination of supporting documents requested by the relevant German embassy or consulate.

Reasonable follow-up and communication of material application updates.

Any regulated legal or immigration advice or representation shall be provided only by an appropriately authorized professional under a separate arrangement where required.

5. EXPENSES NOT INCLUDED

Unless expressly included in a separate written quotation, the €2,000 service fee does not include:

German language school tuition, registration or enrollment fees.

German embassy or consulate visa and appointment fees.

Health insurance, travel insurance, medical examinations or police certificates.

Accommodation, living expenses, local transportation and settlement expenses.

Blocked-account deposits or any other proof-of-funds requirement.

Air tickets, translations, legalization, courier or banking charges.

Any other government, school or third-party charge.

Third-party costs remain payable by the Student directly to the approved recipient unless PARROT gives different written payment instructions. Third-party refund rules are controlled by the relevant provider, not by PARROT.

6. FIRST INSTALLMENT: ADMISSION PROCESS

The first installment of €250 is payable before PARROT begins the admission process. It covers initial assessment, document review, school selection, application preparation and admission coordination.

The first installment shall be refunded if PARROT fails to obtain an official admission or enrollment confirmation, provided that the Student:

Meets the admission requirements disclosed before processing.

Provides all requested documents on time and in the required format.

Provides authentic, accurate and complete information and documents.

Cooperates fully and does not withdraw before completion of the agreed admission process.

Pays any separately disclosed school application charge required for processing.

Where these conditions are met and admission cannot be obtained, PARROT shall process the €250 refund within 15 business days after confirming the unsuccessful admission outcome. Once a genuine admission or enrollment confirmation is obtained, the first installment becomes non-refundable, subject to mandatory law.

An admission letter does not guarantee visa eligibility or visa approval.

7. SECOND INSTALLMENT: VISA-PREPARATION SUPPORT

The second installment of €250 is payable before PARROT begins the agreed visa-document preparation or submission support. Once that work has commenced, the installment is non-refundable, subject to applicable law.

It remains non-refundable if the Student later withdraws, changes travel or study plans, receives a request for additional documents, experiences processing delays, or receives a visa refusal. This does not remove any mandatory refund right arising from PARROT's proven non-performance or material breach.

8. THIRD INSTALLMENT: PAYMENT AFTER VISA APPROVAL

The final installment of €1,500 becomes payable only after the Student receives official or otherwise verifiable approval of the relevant German visa. The Student shall pay the balance within five business days after receiving confirmation of approval. If the visa is refused, the €1,500 installment is not payable.

9. STUDENT RESPONSIBILITIES

Provide a valid passport and all documents requested for admission and visa support.

Provide truthful, complete and consistent information, including prior visa refusals, immigration history, criminal history and relevant medical information when lawfully requested.

Review all forms and documents before signing or submission and promptly correct any error.

Attend interviews, biometric appointments, medical examinations and other appointments as required.

Maintain sufficient funds for tuition, blocked-account requirements, living expenses and all excluded costs.

Follow deadlines and respond promptly to PARROT, the school and German authorities.

Inform PARROT immediately of any change in address, passport, financial situation, school choice or travel plan.

Pay all agreed installments and approved third-party charges when due.

Use only official payment channels and retain every invoice and receipt.

Comply with school rules, visa conditions and applicable laws.

The Student is responsible for consequences arising from false information, hidden refusals, forged documents, missed deadlines, insufficient funds, lack of cooperation or other matters within the Student's control.

10. PARROT RESPONSIBILITIES

Provide accurate information within the agreed service scope.

Prepare and coordinate the admission application with reasonable professional care.

Communicate material school and visa-support requirements and updates.

Maintain reasonable records of work performed and payments received.

Protect the Student's personal information and share it only for legitimate service purposes.

Avoid guaranteeing admission, visa approval, processing time, employment or immigration status.

11. DOCUMENTS AND STUDY REQUIREMENTS

Depending on the Student's circumstances and the current requirements of the school and German authorities, the Student may be required to provide a passport, school admission confirmation, course schedule, proof of finances, blocked-account evidence, insurance, accommodation information, motivation letter, academic or employment history, previous German-language evidence, translations, legalizations and any additional requested document.

Requirements may change. PARROT will communicate known material changes, but the Student remains responsible for meeting the requirements imposed by the school and the relevant authorities.

12. PAYMENT, RECEIPTS AND DEFAULT

The Student shall not pay a staff member's personal account, an unverified third party, cryptocurrency or any recipient not officially approved in writing by PARROT. A payment is recognized only when supported by verifiable proof and acknowledged through an official receipt or record.

If an installment remains unpaid after it becomes due, PARROT may suspend further work after written notice. Suspension does not cancel fees already earned for services performed.

13. WITHDRAWAL, CANCELLATION AND REFUNDS

The Student may cancel by written notice. Any refund shall be determined according to the installment conditions in this Agreement, the services already performed, non-refundable third-party charges and mandatory consumer-protection law.

A refund request must be submitted in writing with the Student's name, agreement reference, reason for the request and proof of payment. PARROT shall review and respond within a reasonable period. Approved refunds shall be paid to the original payer or another lawfully verified recipient.

No refund is due for work properly completed before cancellation, except where this Agreement or mandatory law expressly provides otherwise.

14. NO GUARANTEE

Admission decisions are made by the German language school. Visa decisions and processing times are controlled by German authorities. Admission does not guarantee a visa, and visa approval does not automatically grant employment rights, permanent residence or any future immigration status. PARROT does not guarantee admission, visa approval, employment, a specific processing time or entry into Germany.

15. PROFESSIONAL AND IMMIGRATION BOUNDARIES

PARROT provides education-consulting, referral and administrative-support services within its lawful authority. Where regulated legal or immigration advice or representation is required, the Student may be referred to an appropriately authorized professional under a separate consent or service agreement.

16. CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA PROTECTION

The Student authorizes PARROT to collect, use and share relevant personal information with language schools, authorized professionals and necessary service providers for admission, visa-support and related administration. PARROT shall apply reasonable safeguards and shall not disclose personal information for unrelated purposes without consent, except where disclosure is required by law.

17. COMMUNICATION AND ELECTRONIC ACCEPTANCE

The Student agrees that email, the PARROT MIS, WhatsApp and other designated written channels may be used for notices, invoices, document requests, approvals and status updates. The Student must keep contact information current. Electronic signatures and written electronic acceptance may be treated as valid to the extent permitted by law.

18. TERM AND TERMINATION

This Agreement begins when signed or electronically accepted and continues until the agreed service is completed, cancelled or terminated. PARROT may terminate immediately for fraud, forged documents, abusive conduct, unlawful instructions, misuse of services or material breach. Termination does not remove accrued payment obligations, valid refund rights, confidentiality duties or responsibilities concerning active records.

PARROT is not responsible for independent decisions, delays, policy changes or failures of schools, embassies, consulates, banks, airlines, insurers, accommodation providers, couriers, government authorities or other disclosed third parties, except where PARROT is legally responsible for its own conduct.
