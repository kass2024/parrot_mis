ARTICLE 2: PURPOSE OF THE AGREEMENT
The purpose of this agreement is to establish a professional and long-term B2B partnership for the recruitment, admission processing, and visa application support of international students wishing to travel to Germany to study the German language through face-to-face classes.
This partnership covers exclusively:
Recruitment of eligible students interested in German language studies in Germany.
Selection of suitable German language schools.
Admission applications to German language schools.
Obtaining official admission or enrollment confirmation letters.
Administrative assistance with German language study visa applications.
Communication and follow-up regarding admission and visa applications.
This agreement applies exclusively to in-person German language study programs in Germany.
ARTICLE 3: SCOPE OF SERVICES
PARROT shall provide the following services for eligible students referred by THE PARTNER:
Preliminary assessment of student eligibility.
Identification of suitable German language schools.
Assistance with selecting appropriate German language courses.
Review of academic and identification documents.
Preparation and submission of language school admission applications.
Communication with German language schools.
Follow-up on admission decisions.
Assistance in obtaining official admission or enrollment confirmation letters.
Guidance regarding language course commencement dates and duration.
Administrative support for German visa documentation.
Assistance with preparing visa application checklists.
Communication with THE PARTNER regarding application progress.
Courses may include German language levels A1, A2, B1, B2, C1, or C2, depending on the selected school's offerings.
The Parties acknowledge that the appropriate visa category depends on the course, its intensity and duration, and the applicant's circumstances.
Admission and visa approval are not guaranteed.
ARTICLE 4: SERVICE FEES AND PAYMENT SCHEDULE
For the agreed German language admission and visa application support package, the Parties establish a total service fee of:
€1,200 (ONE THOUSAND TWO HUNDRED EUROS) PER CANDIDATE.
4.1 Payment Schedule
First installment: €125
Payable before starting the German language school admission process.
Second installment: €125
Payable before submitting the German visa application.
Third installment: €950
Payable only after official German visa approval.
TOTAL SERVICE FEE: €1,200 PER CANDIDATE
4.2 Excluded Expenses
The €1,200 service fee does not include the following expenses unless expressly agreed in writing:
German language school tuition fees.
School registration fees.
German embassy visa application fees.
Health insurance.
Accommodation and living expenses.
Blocked account deposits or other required financial evidence.
Flight tickets.
Translation and document legalization fees.
Other government or third-party expenses.
The applicable school and third-party costs shall be disclosed before the candidate commits to them.
ARTICLE 5: FIRST PAYMENT — €125 AND REFUND CONDITIONS
THE PARTNER shall pay the first installment of €125 before PARROT begins the candidate's German language school admission application.
This installment covers preliminary eligibility assessment, document review, school selection, admission preparation, and administrative coordination.
5.1 Refund Eligibility
The first installment of €125 shall be refundable if PARROT fails to obtain an official admission or enrollment confirmation letter from a German language school, provided that:
The candidate meets the admission requirements agreed upon before processing.
All required documents have been submitted correctly.
All submitted information and documents are authentic.
The candidate cooperates throughout the application process.
The unsuccessful outcome is not caused by the candidate's withdrawal, misrepresentation, or failure to meet disclosed requirements.
If PARROT cannot obtain an admission letter under these conditions, the €125 shall be refunded within 15 business days after the unsuccessful admission outcome is confirmed.
Once a valid admission or enrollment confirmation letter is obtained, the first installment becomes non-refundable, except where applicable law requires otherwise.
ARTICLE 6: SECOND PAYMENT — €125 AND NON-REFUNDABLE CONDITIONS
THE PARTNER shall pay the second installment of €125 before submission of the German visa application.
This installment covers agreed visa documentation preparation, administrative review, and application coordination.
6.1 Non-Refundable Condition
The second installment of €125 is non-refundable once the agreed visa preparation services have commenced, subject to applicable law.
This includes circumstances where:
The German visa application is refused.
The candidate voluntarily withdraws after visa preparation begins.
The candidate changes their study or travel plans.
The German embassy requests additional documents.
The visa application takes longer than expected.
This provision does not exclude mandatory refund rights or remedies arising from non-performance, material breach, or applicable consumer protection laws.
ARTICLE 7: THIRD PAYMENT — €950 AFTER VISA APPROVAL
The remaining installment of €950 shall become payable only after the candidate receives official German visa approval.
THE PARTNER agrees to pay this amount within five business days after receiving verifiable confirmation of visa approval.
If the German visa application is refused, the final €950 installment shall not become payable.
Visa approval must be supported by official or otherwise verifiable confirmation from the relevant German authority.
ARTICLE 8: GERMAN LANGUAGE SCHOOL ADMISSION AND VISA REQUIREMENTS
The Parties acknowledge that candidates may be required to provide:
A valid international passport.
A completed German language school application.
Academic certificates or educational background information where requested.
An official admission or enrollment confirmation letter.
Details of the German language course, including schedule and duration.
Proof of sufficient financial resources.
Proof of appropriate health insurance.
Accommodation information, where required.
A motivation letter explaining the purpose of learning German.
Any additional documents required by the selected language school or German embassy.
The exact requirements shall depend on the selected program, visa category, applicant's nationality and residence, and applicable German immigration rules.
ARTICLE 9: RESPONSIBILITIES OF PARROT
PARROT agrees to:
Provide accurate information regarding available German language courses.
Assess candidates' preliminary eligibility.
Identify suitable German language schools.
Prepare and coordinate admission applications.
Communicate admission requirements and document checklists.
Follow up on admission applications.

Avoid guaranteeing admission or visa approval.
ARTICLE 10: RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE PARTNER
STUDY AND WORK ABROAD EXPERT LTD. agrees to:
Recruit and refer eligible students interested in studying German in Germany.
Explain the nature of face-to-face German language courses.
Submit genuine and complete candidate documents.
Provide accurate candidate information.
Explain applicable admission and visa requirements to referred students.
Pay the agreed service fees according to Article 4.
Obtain appropriate client consent before sharing personal information.
Avoid misleading statements or guarantees of visa approval.
Cooperate with PARROT regarding pending applications.
Comply with applicable education, immigration, and consumer protection laws.
Maintain professional communication with PARROT and referred students.
Inform students that German language study does not automatically guarantee employment or permanent residence in Germany.

ARTICLE 13: CONFIDENTIALITY AND DATA PROTECTION
Both Parties shall protect confidential information received during the partnership, including:
Candidate identity documents.
Academic records.
Financial documentation.
German language school admission records.
Visa application documentation.
Business correspondence.
Internal commercial information not publicly disclosed.
Personal information shall be collected, used, stored, and shared only for lawful and authorized purposes.
The Parties shall obtain necessary consent before transferring candidate information to language schools, authorized professionals, or other third parties.
ARTICLE 14: PROFESSIONAL AND REGULATORY COMPLIANCE
The Parties acknowledge that immigration advice and representation may be regulated activities.
Neither Party shall provide regulated German immigration advice or representation unless legally authorized to do so.
Where necessary, regulated services shall be referred to appropriately authorized professionals.
Nothing in this agreement shall override applicable immigration laws, professional requirements, or candidate rights.
ARTICLE 15: NO GUARANTEE OF OUTCOME
Both Parties acknowledge that:
Admission decisions are made by the relevant German language schools.
Visa decisions are made by the competent German authorities.
Admission does not guarantee visa approval.
Visa processing times may vary.
A language course admission or visa does not automatically guarantee employment rights or permanent residence in Germany.
Neither Party shall advertise or promise guaranteed admission or visa approval.
ARTICLE 16: EXCLUSIVE SCOPE OF SERVICES
This agreement applies only to candidates travelling to Germany to attend in-person German language courses.
The following services are expressly excluded:
Canadian immigration and permanent residence services.
Canadian work permit applications.
Employment recruitment and job placement.
University degree admissions unrelated to the agreed language course.
Online-only German language courses.
Immigration and visa services for other countries.
Other international education or employment services.
Any additional service shall require a separate written agreement signed by both Parties.
ARTICLE 17: NON-EXCLUSIVITY
Unless otherwise agreed in writing, this agreement is non-exclusive.
Each Party may cooperate with other legally registered educational institutions or service providers.
Both Parties shall respect confidentiality obligations and client-specific commitments established under this agreement.
ARTICLE 18: DURATION AND TERMINATION
This agreement shall enter into force on the date it is signed by both Parties.
The initial duration shall be one year.
The agreement may be renewed by mutual written consent.
Either Party may terminate the agreement by providing 30 days' written notice.
Earlier termination may occur in cases of serious breach, fraud, unlawful conduct, or material non-compliance, subject to applicable law.
Termination shall not eliminate:
Outstanding lawful payment obligations.
Accrued refund rights.
Confidentiality obligations.
Responsibilities relating to applications already accepted for processing.
The Parties shall cooperate to avoid unnecessary disruption to active student applications.
ARTICLE 19: INDEPENDENT CONTRACTORS
PARROT and THE PARTNER remain independent legal entities.
Nothing in this agreement creates an employment relationship, joint venture, separate legal partnership, or unrestricted authority for either Party to bind the other.
Each Party remains responsible for its own business operations, staff, taxes, and regulatory compliance.
