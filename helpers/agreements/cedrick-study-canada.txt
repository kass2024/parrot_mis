1  Purpose and Appointment
The Client appoints the Company to provide education-consulting, admission coordination, document administration, study-permit application support, payment coordination, client-funds administration and pre-departure support for the Client's proposed studies in Canada, subject to this Agreement. The Company accepts the appointment within the limits of its lawful authority.
The purpose of the funds-administration arrangement is to ensure that funds assigned to the study plan are used only for approved education and application expenses, supported by records and protected from unauthorized use until Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) issues a decision or this Agreement lawfully ends.
2  Scope of Services
Review the Client's academic profile, study objectives, travel and immigration history, financial plan and available documents.
Recommend suitable Canadian institutions and programs and prepare or coordinate approved admission applications.
Collect, organize and quality-check academic, identity, financial, employment, sponsorship and supporting documents.
Follow up on admission decisions, conditions, tuition deposits, letters of acceptance, provincial or territorial attestation requirements and deferrals where applicable.
Prepare administrative forms, document lists, explanations, submission records and application trackers for the study-permit process.
Coordinate regulated Canadian immigration advice or representation only through an appropriately authorized Canadian professional under a separate retainer or written authorization when required.
Maintain payment, receipt and disbursement records and provide the Client with statements showing how the CAD 4,000 package is allocated under Schedule A.
Monitor communications and requests received after submission and promptly inform the Client of material updates, deadlines and the final decision.
Provide reasonable pre-departure guidance after approval, excluding airfare, accommodation, settlement and living costs unless separately agreed in writing.
3  Complete Inclusive Package Fee
The Client shall pay CAD 4,000 in full before the Company begins chargeable work. This is the complete inclusive package price for the agreed process in Section 2 through the first IRCC decision. The Company shall issue an official receipt and shall not request another registration, admission-application, tuition-deposit, financial-document preparation, consultation, processing, document-preparation, follow-up or success fee for that process.
The package includes CAD 450 for registration and admission applications; the tuition deposit actually required by the selected institution, from CAD 500 up to CAD 3,000; approved costs of preparing financial documents; and the remaining professional, administrative and standard application costs. These are components of the same CAD 4,000 and are not payable again by the Client. The fee does not guarantee admission, a study permit or entry to Canada.

4  Costs Included in the CAD 4,000 Package
The CAD 4,000 package includes the stated CAD 450 registration and admission-application amount, the actual tuition deposit from CAD 500 to CAD 3,000, approved financial-document preparation, and the normal professional and administrative work required for the agreed process. Standard mandatory application disbursements shown in Schedule A are also administered within the package and may not be presented to the Client as an additional Company charge.
The Company shall record the actual allocation of the package in Schedule A and keep the relevant school, government or supplier receipts. If the tuition deposit required by the selected institution falls within the agreed CAD 500 to CAD 3,000 range, the Company shall pay it from the package without requesting an additional payment from the Client.
5  Inclusive Package Funds Administration
Until an included amount is lawfully paid to an approved school, government body or supplier, the Company shall account for it as part of the Client's package allocation. The Company shall not divert an allocated tuition deposit, registration amount or other included disbursement to an unrelated purpose.
Schedule A shall identify the package components, intended recipients, actual amounts, payment triggers and supporting records.
The Company shall use the package only for the agreed process and shall not divert an amount allocated to registration, applications, tuition deposit or financial-document preparation to an unrelated purpose.
The Company shall maintain a transaction ledger for the Client showing the date, amount, currency, recipient, purpose, authorization and supporting receipt for every movement of funds.
The Company shall provide a statement on reasonable request and after every material disbursement, refusal, withdrawal, termination or final reconciliation.
No cash withdrawal, transfer to an employee or agent, currency conversion, commission, deduction or payment to an undisclosed third party is permitted without written Client authorization and a supporting record.
If a school, government body or supplier issues a refund, the Company shall record it and return or reapply it only in accordance with the Client's written direction, subject to the third party's refund rules.
Any bank, transfer or foreign-exchange charge must be disclosed in advance or shown on the actual transaction record; no undisclosed markup is permitted.
6  Safeguarding and Payment Method
The Company shall make included payments from the CAD 4,000 package directly to the approved school, government body or supplier whenever practical. The receiving account, authorized signatories, safeguarding method and recordkeeping process must be identified in Schedule A. The Company shall not describe an account as a trust account unless it is legally established and operated as such.
The Client shall not send funds to a staff member's personal account, an unverified agent, a cryptocurrency address or any account not stated in an official Company payment instruction. A payment is recognized only after the Company issues an official receipt or written confirmation.
7  No Other Process Charges and Travel Preparation Costs
Except for the CAD 4,000 inclusive package, the Company shall not charge the Client another amount for the agreed process through the first IRCC decision. After approval, the Client remains responsible for personal travel and settlement costs, including airfare, baggage, accommodation, food, local transportation, telephone, clothing, personal insurance and living expenses in Canada.
A later optional service, such as airport pickup, accommodation placement, settlement support, a new application after refusal, a change of institution or program, or work outside Section 2, requires a separate written agreement and is not payable unless the Client freely signs that agreement.
8  Budget Approval and Spending Authority
Schedule A is the controlling budget. The Company may make only the listed payments when the stated trigger occurs. A material change in amount, recipient or purpose requires the Client's prior written approval. An urgent deadline does not authorize the Company to spend outside the approved budget without consent.
The Client may revoke a future payment authorization in writing before the payment is irreversibly processed. Revocation does not reverse a payment already made to a third party and remains subject to that recipient's rules.
9  Client Responsibilities
Provide complete, accurate and authentic information and documents and disclose all prior visa refusals, immigration history, criminal matters, medical issues and material changes.
Review all applications, forms, statements and supporting documents before submission and confirm their accuracy in writing.
Maintain sufficient lawful proof of funds for the study-permit application and sufficient personal funds for airfare and living and settlement expenses in Canada, and provide credible evidence of their source and availability.
Respond promptly to document requests, school conditions, government requests, interview notices, medical instructions and deadlines.
Never provide false, altered, borrowed or misleading documents and never conceal information requested by a school or government authority.
Keep contact information current and protect portal credentials, payment instructions and personal information.
10  Immigration Advice and Authorized Representation
The Company acts as an education consultant and administrative service provider. It does not provide regulated Canadian immigration advice or representation unless the service is delivered by an authorized Canadian representative. When regulated advice or representation is required, the Client may be referred to a Regulated Canadian Immigration Consultant, lawyer, notary or other authorized professional under a separate retainer or written consent.
The Client is not required to appoint a representative. The Client retains the right to review and approve every submission and to communicate directly with the school and government authorities, subject to applicable portal and representation rules.
11  No Guarantee and Decision-Making Authority
The Company does not guarantee admission, a scholarship, a provincial or territorial attestation letter, a study permit, processing time, interview outcome, refund, entry to Canada or any other result. Schools, IRCC, border officials, medical providers and other third parties make their own decisions and may change requirements or timelines.
12  Decision, Reconciliation and Return of Unused Funds
Within ten business days after the Company receives the final IRCC decision, the Client withdraws, or this Agreement ends, the Company shall prepare a reconciliation statement showing: (a) the CAD 4,000 received; (b) each package allocation and payment; (c) refunds received; (d) documented bank or transfer charges; and (e) any unearned and unused balance.
Subject to clearance of payments, third-party refund timelines and any lawful hold, the Company shall return any unearned and unused package balance within ten business days after reconciliation or within ten business days after a delayed third-party refund is actually received. Funds shall be returned to the Client or verified original source unless law or fraud-prevention controls require another documented method.
13  Service-Fee Earning and Refund
The CAD 4,000 package is allocated and earned progressively as work is completed and included costs become non-refundable. If this Agreement ends before completion, the Company may retain documented work already performed and non-recoverable payments already made to schools, government bodies or suppliers; any remaining unearned and unused balance must be refunded with an itemized statement.

14  Receipts, Records and Audit Trail
The Company shall issue an official receipt for every payment received, preserve supporting records for each authorized disbursement, and make the Client's ledger reasonably available. The Client must report any discrepancy within fifteen business days after receiving a statement, but failure to do so does not validate fraud or an unlawful transaction.
15  Confidentiality and Data Protection
The Company shall use the Client's personal, academic, financial, employment, passport, immigration and medical information only for authorized services, legal compliance, fraud prevention and records administration. Disclosure is limited to staff, schools, partners, financial institutions, government authorities and authorized professionals who reasonably need the information for the agreed process.
Each party shall protect credentials and confidential information and promptly report a suspected loss, unauthorized access, altered payment instruction or cyber incident. These duties survive termination.
16  Termination and File Handover
Either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice. The Company shall stop future discretionary spending, protect pending deadlines, provide the Client with a reasonable copy of the file, complete the reconciliation in Section 12 and return any unearned and unused package balance. Termination does not cancel an irreversible third-party payment or completed work.
17  Complaints and Dispute Resolution
The Client should first submit a written complaint to the Company at infos@visaconsultantcanada.com. The Company shall acknowledge it within five business days and provide a substantive response within fifteen business days where reasonably possible. The parties shall attempt good-faith negotiation and may use mediation before court proceedings.
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Rwanda, subject to any mandatory rights that apply to a payment, regulated professional or service performed elsewhere. Nothing in this Agreement removes either party's right to approach a competent regulator, authority or court.
18  Notices and Changes
Notices must be delivered by hand, acknowledged email, registered mail or another verifiable method to the contact details above. Any change to the CAD 4,000 fee, service scope, budget, payment recipient, refund terms or funds-administration authority is valid only if written and signed or electronically accepted by both parties.
19  Entire Agreement and Severability
This Agreement and its signed schedules contain the entire agreement on the stated services and replace earlier oral or written promises on the same subject. If a provision is invalid or unenforceable, it shall be limited only to the extent necessary and the remaining provisions continue. Mandatory legal rights cannot be waived.
